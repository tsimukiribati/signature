MOE Staff Email Signature

Make your official Ministry email signature in two minutes.

Preview copy. When this page is hosted on the TSIMU website, the logo is copied with the signature automatically and the extra logo steps disappear.

1Type your details

2Check and copy your signature

3Add it to Gmail on your computer

  1. Open Gmail. Click the gear icon at the top right, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll down to Signature. Click Create new, type MOE and click Create.
  3. Click inside the empty box and press Ctrl+V. On a Mac press Cmd+V.
  4. Add the logo: click in front of the words MOE LOGO, click the picture icon, choose Google Drive, pick MOE_Logo_Signature_90px.png and click Select. Delete the words MOE LOGO, then click the logo and choose Original size.
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose MOE for For new emails use and for On reply/forward use.
  6. Scroll to the very bottom of the page and click Save Changes. Gmail does not keep your signature until you click Save Changes.
  7. Send a test email to yourself and check that the logo and your details show.

4On your phone (optional)

The Gmail app only takes plain text. In the app, open Settings, tap your MOE account, then Mobile signature, and paste this.
