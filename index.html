MOE Staff Email Signature

Make your official Ministry email signature in two minutes.

Preview copy. When this page is hosted on the TSIMU website, the logo is copied with the signature automatically and the extra logo steps disappear.

1Type your details

Use this when helping another person on the same computer.

2Check and copy your signature

Gmail did not open? Click here to open Gmail settings.

3Paste it in Gmail

First click the red button Copy my signature and open Gmail in step 2.
  1. In Gmail, scroll down to Signature. Click + Create new, type MOE and click Create. If Gmail shows your inbox instead, click the gear icon at the top right, then See all settings, and scroll down.
  2. Click inside the empty box and press Ctrl+V to paste. On a Mac press Cmd+V. Your signature and the logo appear. Something else appeared? Delete it, come back to this page, click Copy again below, then paste again.
  3. Add the logo: click in front of the words MOE LOGO, click the picture icon, choose Google Drive, pick MOE_Logo_Signature_90px.png and click Select. Delete the words MOE LOGO, then click the logo and choose Original size.
  4. Just below, under Signature defaults, set both boxes to MOE, so your signature goes on new emails and on replies: For new emails useMOE On reply/forward useMOE Insert signature before quoted text in replies and remove the "--" line that precedes it Then tick the box shown above. The tick puts your signature right under what you write in a reply, not at the bottom of the old messages.
  5. Scroll to the very bottom of the page and click Save Changes. Gmail does not keep your signature until you click Save Changes.
  6. Test it: send a new email to yourself, then open it and click Reply. Your signature must show in the new email and in the reply. No signature in the reply? Go back to Signature defaults and check that On reply/forward use says MOE, then click Save Changes again.

4On your phone (optional)

The Gmail app only takes plain text. In the app, open Settings, tap your MOE account, then Mobile signature, and paste this.
